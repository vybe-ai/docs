title: Cancel subscription
icon: circle-xmark
---

Customers can cancel their ongoing VYDEO subscriptions on their account page on the [VYDEO website.](https://vydeo.app/settings?tab=billing)

### Frequently asked questions
<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="What happens when I cancel my subscription?">
    When you cancel your subscription, your subscription will still be active until the end of the current billing period. 
    After the current billing period, you will no longer be charged for VYDEO.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
